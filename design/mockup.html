1 · Clawd, the character

Original pixel art on a 16×10 grid: square terracotta body, block eyes, stubby arms, four legs. Three body tones give it the chunky 3D-pixel look from your references. Animations step frame by frame (no smooth tweening), so he moves like a sprite.

2 · Design language

Warm, dark glass by default with one accent: Clawd's terracotta, used only for checks, progress, focus and the main button. A pixel font for small labels and numbers echoes Clawd; a clean sans for everything you read.

3 · Laptop: Clawd floating, list open

Clawd sits on top of every window. Tap him and the glass list opens on whichever side has room; he switches to typing while it's open. Drag him anywhere. The panel blurs whatever is behind it (here, a code editor and the wallpaper).
